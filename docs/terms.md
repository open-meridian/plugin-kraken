# Terms

A summary, not legal advice. Read Kraken's terms yourself before relying on
this plugin.

## Kraken's terms

Kraken asks its prior permission, at marketdata@kraken.com, for any
non-personal commercial use of the data from its public endpoints. Without
that permission, Kraken's data is for one person's own use.

Each of the plugin's datasets describes those terms in words on the Data
sources page, and declares no licence: your deployment's licence there is
the one enforced, and the page warns when more than one person reads a
plugin entitled to a dataset licensed on one person's terms. The plugin and its documentation never
say that a deployment meets Kraken's terms.

## Your due diligence

Whether your use of Kraken's data is within its terms is yours to decide:
who sees the prices, whether an agent's client passes what it reads to its
model's provider, and whether anything leaves your organisation.

## Your data

The plugin keeps Kraken's answers in your deployment's storage and sends
nothing anywhere but to the lake through its sidecar. It sends Kraken
nothing but its requests for public market data.
